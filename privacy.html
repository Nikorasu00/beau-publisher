Beau Publisher Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Beau Publisher is an in-house tool used by the owner of the Instagram accounts @beau_horroranimation and @beau_tificationidn to publish their own posts, read the comments on their own posts and reply to those comments. It is not offered to other businesses or the public.

What we collect

When you comment on a post or reel published by our accounts, Beau Publisher may read your comment ID, the comment text and your Instagram username through the Instagram API. If your comment contains a keyword such as "lanjut", "part 2" or "cerita", we send you one private reply and Meta returns an Instagram-scoped ID for that message.

How we use it

We use this information only to decide whether to send you the private reply you asked for (for example the link to the next episode) and to make sure you never get the same reply twice. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and retention

The information is stored in a local file on the account owner's computer, not on a server. Entries are deleted after 30 days.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer your information to any third party.

Your choices and deletion

You can delete your comment at any time on Instagram. To ask us to delete the information we hold about you, send a direct message to @beau_horroranimation on Instagram. We remove it within 7 days. Details: data deletion instructions.

Contact

Questions about this policy: send a direct message to @beau_horroranimation on Instagram.
