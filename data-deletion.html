Data deletion instructions

Beau Publisher · last updated 18 November 2026

Beau Publisher keeps only the comment ID, comment text, Instagram username and message ID needed to reply once to a comment on our own Instagram posts. Entries are deleted automatically after 30 days.

To have your data deleted sooner:

  1. Send a direct message to @beau_horroranimation on Instagram with the words "hapus data" (delete my data).
  2. We delete every entry linked to your Instagram username within 7 days and reply to confirm.

You can also delete your comment on Instagram at any time. See our privacy policy.
